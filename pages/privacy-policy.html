Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website belongs to Om Namah Shivay Travels ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains, in plain language, how information you share with us through this website is used.

This Website Has No Backend

This is a static website. It does not have a server-side database, and no form on this site submits data to us over the internet automatically. When you fill in the Contact or Hire Us form and press submit, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details already typed into a message — the message is sent to us only if you choose to press send inside WhatsApp. We never see or store your form entries unless you complete that WhatsApp message yourself.

Information We Receive

  • Details you choose to send us on WhatsApp, by phone, or by email — such as your name, mobile number, email address and travel plans.
  • Basic, non-identifying technical information that any web host or browser may collect automatically, such as general device or browser type.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and help plan your tour.
  • To share relevant tour information you've asked about.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Payments and Sensitive Data

We do not collect passwords, card numbers or payment details through this website. Any payment for a tour is arranged directly with our team through a separate, secure conversation — never through a form on this site.

Third-Party Services

This site links out to WhatsApp, and may embed a Google Map or link to YouTube videos. These third-party services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or browser storage to collect personal data.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out through the contact details on our Contact Us page.
