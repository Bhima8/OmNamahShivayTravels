About Om Namah Shivay Travels

A decade of planning pilgrimage and holiday journeys from Solapur, built on trust and steady care.

Our Story

Om Namah Shivay Travels began with a simple idea: pilgrimage and holiday travel should feel peaceful, not stressful. What started as a small Solapur-based operation arranging local temple trips has grown into a full tour planning service — covering Jyotirlinga pilgrimages, Himalayan yatras, Konkan getaways and family holidays across the country.

Every itinerary we plan is shaped by the same idea: take care of the logistics so travelers can focus on the experience — the darshan, the mountain air, the time with family.
